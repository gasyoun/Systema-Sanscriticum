- **H3821 (Sonnet 5 `claude-sonnet-5`, 01-09-2026): published fixed EUR/USD PayPal price list, ruled by H3819's reconciliation ([Uprava docs/PAYPAL_RECONCILIATION_MONEY_CONTOUR_01-09-2026.md](https://github.com/gasyoun/Uprava/blob/main/docs/PAYPAL_RECONCILIATION_MONEY_CONTOUR_01-09-2026.md), [5/5 vote](https://gasyoun.github.io/vote/sheets/h3819_paypal_price_list.html), [PR #2315](https://github.com/gasyoun/Systema-Sanscriticum/pull/2315)).** The reconciliation found the same RUB tariff quoted 0–18% apart to different PayPal payers, because `services.paypal.foreign_block_prices` was a hand-maintained config array (block tariffs only). New [`PaypalForeignPriceService`](https://github.com/gasyoun/Systema-Sanscriticum/blob/main/app/Services/Payments/PaypalForeignPriceService.php) computes `round(tariff.price / fx_rate * 1.08, 2)` — `fx_rate` from the **existing** `CurrencyRateProvider` (exchangerate.host, previously only used for teacher payout conversion) — and publishes it into `tariff_foreign_prices`, refreshed monthly by `php artisan paypal:refresh-foreign-prices` (`--dry-run` for the human-review report before flipping the flag). **Carve-out (MG ruling):** a payer with an active `student_discounts` row for the tariff's course/block is excluded from the 8% markup — their price is the already-discounted RUB amount at the pure fx_rate. Flag `features.paypal_fixed_price_list` (`PAYPAL_FIXED_PRICE_LIST_ENABLED`) stays **OFF by default** — `PaypalClaimController::show()` reproduces the pre-existing config-array behavior bit-for-bit while dark, verified by a dedicated regression test. 7/7 new unit tests (markup formula across all 7 live RUB tariff blocks, both discount types, inactive-discount non-exclusion, refresh-vs-dry-run, missing-fx-rate skip, never touches `payments`/`payment_audits`) + 3 new feature tests; full `Tariff|Checkout|StudentDiscount` regression sweep 169/169 green.

Parent: H5285. 4. Human review: draft glosses go to review sheets of 10 cards or fewer (grouped by pack/locus); only approved glosses land.

Acceptance: `status: empty` + `not_russian` on lemmas_for_srs down by at least 80%, every landed gloss either from our data or approved on a sheet.

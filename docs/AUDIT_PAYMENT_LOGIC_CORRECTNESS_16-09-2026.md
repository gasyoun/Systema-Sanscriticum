# Payment logic correctness audit — 16-09-2026

_Created: 16-09-2026 · Last updated: 16-09-2026_

**Question asked (MG, 16-09-2026): «Payment logic is perfect?»** Short answer: **no.** The Tochka card path is solid (signature, `event_hash` idempotency, hold≠capture, resurrection and amount-tolerance guards, all default ON). The problems cluster in four places that the Tochka guards never covered: the PayPal manual-claim form, the PayPal subscriptions webhook, promise/refund lifecycle, and teacher payout arithmetic.

Method: four independent read-only reviewers (Explore lane, Fable 5.1 `claude-fable-5-1` orchestrating) over the checkout, webhook, lifecycle and payout paths; every HIGH below was re-read from source by the orchestrator, and the three flag-dependent ones were probed on prod `.env` (read-only `grep`, 16-09-2026). Payment-related test run: **not completed on this box** — `php artisan test --filter=Payment|Paypal|…` died twice at PHP's 128 MB `memory_limit` (Fatal in Carbon autoload, not a test failure), and the 1 GB rerun sat at 0.6 % CPU for 110 min under a load average of 130–163 (52 concurrent Claude sessions) and was killed. No test failure was observed; no test pass is claimed either. Rerun on the Windows box or a quiet Mac: `php -d memory_limit=1G vendor/bin/phpunit --filter='Payment|Paypal|Checkout|Refund|Deposit|Promise|Payout|Salary|Tariff|Debt'`.

## Findings — MEDIUM

1. **Failed→paid late checkout keeps refunded credit.** `pending→failed` refunds prana and referral credit; a bank link paid after `ExpireStaleCheckouts` marked it failed transitions `failed→paid` at the discounted amount with nothing re-deducted. [Payment.php:777-779](https://github.com/gasyoun/Systema-Sanscriticum/blob/main/app/Models/Payment.php#L777-L779), [WebhookController.php:144-151](https://github.com/gasyoun/Systema-Sanscriticum/blob/main/app/Http/Controllers/WebhookController.php#L144-L151).
2. **`payment_link_expires_at` is written, never enforced at pay time.** Zero reads in `WebhookController`; a promo order paid after TTL still redeems, so `used_count` can exceed `usage_limit`.
3. **Promo applied after deposit credit** shrinks the advertised discount (8000 price, 5000 deposit, −50 % promo ⇒ 6500 effective, not 4000). [Tariff.php:266-271](https://github.com/gasyoun/Systema-Sanscriticum/blob/main/app/Models/Tariff.php#L266-L271), [PaymentController.php:266](https://github.com/gasyoun/Systema-Sanscriticum/blob/main/app/Http/Controllers/PaymentController.php#L266). May be intended; nothing states the order.
4. **Supplement claim matches any pending payment with `amount == 2000.00`** on the course and force-flips it `paid` `withoutEvents`. [PaypalClaimController.php:198-205](https://github.com/gasyoun/Systema-Sanscriticum/blob/main/app/Http/Controllers/PaypalClaimController.php#L198-L205).
5. **Refunds are unbounded**: no cap against the original, no double-refund guard, the candidate list does not exclude the record itself; `deferredRevenueAsOf` sums `ABS(amount)` of refund rows of any status. [PaymentResource.php:260-275](https://github.com/gasyoun/Systema-Sanscriticum/blob/main/app/Filament/Resources/PaymentResource.php#L260-L275).
6. **A refund never re-evaluates access**; a fully refunded student keeps groups and lesson keys. Deleting a paid payment (bulk delete is enabled, no soft-deletes) or editing its `amount` re-syncs revenue only: deposits stay consumed, fulfilled promises keep a dangling link.
7. **`canceled → paid` re-fires `fireOnPaid`** from Filament edit / `confirmPaypal` / `confirmBankSepa`; `hasPriorPaidTransition()` is consulted only in the Tochka webhook. [Payment.php:770-776](https://github.com/gasyoun/Systema-Sanscriticum/blob/main/app/Models/Payment.php#L770-L776).
8. **Cancelling a promise never calls `revokeForPromise()`** (relation manager, Debtors page, upcoming-promises widget). Mitigated on prod: `CONDITIONAL_ACCESS_EXPIRY=true` is set there, so the read-side expiry guard is live; the `paid` conditional rows still linger.
9. **`grantForPromise` never checks `promise->status`** — grant on a cancelled promise from the Debtors page is possible. [ConditionalAccessGranter.php:41-89](https://github.com/gasyoun/Systema-Sanscriticum/blob/main/app/Services/ConditionalAccessGranter.php#L41-L89).
10. **Webhook signature kill switch** `services.paypal.subscriptions.skip_signature_verify` is honoured at runtime with no `app.env` guard; the only check is a deploy-time artisan preflight. Unset on prod today. [HttpPaypalWebhookSignatureVerifier.php:24-26](https://github.com/gasyoun/Systema-Sanscriticum/blob/main/app/Services/Payments/HttpPaypalWebhookSignatureVerifier.php#L24-L26).
11. **Unhandled PayPal events burn the replay key** (`DECISION_UNMATCHED` under `hash(event_id)`), so adding refund support later cannot reprocess history. Generic `Throwable` → 500 forever; rejected charges hash with `uniqid()`, so retries append ledger rows without bound.
12. **Refund lines re-deducted on every payout recalculation** (dedup applies to positive lines only; `keptTotal` not floored). [TeacherSalaryService.php:672-700](https://github.com/gasyoun/Systema-Sanscriticum/blob/main/app/Services/TeacherSalaryService.php#L672-L700), [PayoutRunService.php:146-174](https://github.com/gasyoun/Systema-Sanscriticum/blob/main/app/Services/PayoutRunService.php#L146-L174).
13. **`payable_eur` ignores advances** while `payable_rub` deducts them — two truths in one payout row. [PayoutRunService.php:234-248](https://github.com/gasyoun/Systema-Sanscriticum/blob/main/app/Services/PayoutRunService.php#L234-L248).
14. **Direct receipts marked consumed at a zero exchange rate** offset nothing but are snapshotted as settled; a negative gross burns a settlement act for nothing. [TeacherSalaries.php:609-629](https://github.com/gasyoun/Systema-Sanscriticum/blob/main/app/Filament/Pages/TeacherSalaries.php#L609-L629).
15. **No payout-run idempotency**: no unique `(teacher_id, period)` index; a double submit creates two payouts and two mirror `salary_payout` payments.

## LOW

Float `== 0` on the final price ([PaymentController.php:408](https://github.com/gasyoun/Systema-Sanscriticum/blob/main/app/Http/Controllers/PaymentController.php#L408)) and a float `>=` on deposit full-consumption ([Payment.php:1287](https://github.com/gasyoun/Systema-Sanscriticum/blob/main/app/Models/Payment.php#L1287)) while the reversal path correctly uses integer cents; `payments.transaction_id` has no unique index (application-level `FOR UPDATE` only); `PaymentImportService` creates `paid` rows `withoutEvents` and never consumes deposits or fulfils promises.

## Verified correct (so nobody re-audits it)

Tochka webhook: signature, `event_hash` unique + `firstOrCreate` inside the transaction, hold≠capture, resurrection guard, amount tolerance. Checkout: price always from `Tariff`, promo re-resolved server-side, `prana_amount` recomputed and snapped, `max(0, …)` clamps, duplicate-pending guards with row locks, inactive-tariff guard ON by default. Deposits: `take = min(residual, remaining)` never over-consumes; reversal in integer cents; double-spend across two pending orders blocked. Promise fulfilment via `PromiseFulfillment::fulfil` re-reads under lock and rejects a second fulfilment. Payouts: single `Money::round` at boundaries, RUB source of truth, `paid()`/`real()` scopes exclude canceled/pending/conditional, advance FIFO in a transaction with teacher lock. PayPal webhook: amount **and** currency compared against a provider-currency expectation, fail-closed when absent; user from `commitment->user_id`, never from payload email; invalid signature → 401 before any write.

## What the existing `AuditCheckoutIntegrity` command covers

Negative referral wallets, stranded deposit credit, promo counter drift, legacy null-expiry pending links, rejected webhook deliveries, paid-but-no-group. It covers **none** of H1–H8.

## Suggested fix order (a human should decide the policy ones)

1. H1+H2 together (one PR): compare the claimed amount to `PaypalForeignPriceService::priceFor()` with the same ±0.5 tolerance the supplement branch uses, and add an existing-paid/duplicate-txn guard. Policy question for MG: keep `PAYPAL_TRUST_EXISTING_STUDENTS=true` at all once the amount is checked, or drop trusted-claim to `pending` when the amount mismatches.
2. H5 (one-line guard + a two-promise test): set `fulfilled_payment_id` only on the first promise, `null` on the rest, as `PromiseAutoFulfiller` already does.
3. H6+H12 (payout window): make the two predicates exclusive (`at <= since.endOfDay()` for prior, `at > since.endOfDay()` for window) and dedup return lines.
4. H7: reuse `settleAdvancesForBlockPayout` FIFO logic in the manual payout action, wrap in `DB::transaction`.
5. H3+H4: handle REFUNDED/REVERSED/DENIED, and gate status upgrades on `resource.update_time` or on current status (`Cancelled` never re-Activates from UPDATED).
6. H8: policy — set `GRANT_ACCESS_FAIL_CLOSED=true` on prod as the H2085 decision already ruled, or record why not.
